# Kuaidian Reading (快点阅读)

> Kuaidian Reading is a Chinese mobile dialogue-fiction reading community operated by Beijing Tiantong Interactive Technology Co., Ltd. (Sky Platanus Interactive, 天桐互动). The app publishes chat-style, dialogue-format novels rather than long-form web fiction, distributed by a decentralized recommendation algorithm, with barrage comments, reader tipping and a co-writing mode for new authors. It is a consumer product; as of this profile it publishes no public API, SDK, or developer program.

## Status

- No public API: no `api.`, `developer.`, or `open.` subdomain resolves; no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, or webhook surface is documented.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery documents (security.txt, openid-configuration, oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json all 404).
- No published vulnerability-disclosure program or trust center.
- Only the consumer web/app surface and legal pages are public.
